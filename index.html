The 4-Step Collaborative Pipeline

How SkillBridge verifies competency, matches candidates, and automates hiring.

1

Proctored Assessment

Students take assessments with two-factor timers, webcam face verification, and tab-switch integrity checks.

2

Skill Score Calibration

Test results and coding footprint (GitHub/LeetCode) are normalized into a verifiable 1000-point skill vector.

3

Transparent Matching Gate

Students view exactly where they meet employer requirements (≥ required score) and receive gap remediation for deficits.

4

Direct Shortlist & Offer

Eligible candidates complete a 20-question proctored screening test to deliver their resume directly to the employer.
