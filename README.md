## 📌 About The Project

**SkillBridge** is an Academia–Industry Collaboration Platform designed to reduce the gap between the skills students possess and the skills industries actually require.

Traditional job and internship platforms mainly focus on **listing opportunities**.

SkillBridge focuses on a different question:

> **"Does this opportunity actually match the student's skills?"**

Students build their skill profiles through assessments instead of relying only on self-declared skills. Companies define the skills required for their internships and jobs. The platform then compares both sides and helps students understand where they fit — and where they need to improve.

The goal is not to create another job portal.

The goal is to create a **skill-to-opportunity bridge**.

---

# 🎯 The Problem

Students, companies and academic institutions face different problems that are connected to the same skill gap.

### 👨‍🎓 Students

- Often apply to opportunities without knowing whether they are actually eligible.
- Have difficulty identifying which skills are required for a particular career path.
- May know a technology but have no measurable indication of their current proficiency.
- Receive limited feedback when they are not selected.

### 🏢 Companies

- Receive large numbers of applications.
- Spend time filtering candidates based on resumes and stated skills.
- Have difficulty identifying candidates who actually match their technical requirements.

### 🏫 Institutions

- Have limited visibility into the actual skill readiness of students.
- Mostly track placement outcomes rather than the skills leading to those outcomes.
- Need better insight into industry-required skills and student skill gaps.
